# Cola Battlegrounds

A simple Unity game where the eternal battle between Pepsi and Cola finally explodes into all-out war.

## About

In **Cola Battlegrounds**, players choose a side and battle it out in a fizzy fight for carbonated dominance. Will Pepsi reign supreme, or will Cola crush the competition?

## Features

- Choose your soda faction
- Soda-powered weapons and effects
- Basic enemy AI
- Explosive carbonated combat

## How to Play

1. Launch the game from Unity or the build folder.
2. Select your team: Pepsi or Cola.
3. Use WASD to move, mouse to aim and shoot.
4. Eliminate the enemy and claim soda supremacy!
